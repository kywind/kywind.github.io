About

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student at Columbia, as part of the Columbia Core AI Lab (CAIL), advised by Yunzhu Li.

My main research interest is robot learning, with a focus on understanding the role of physics simulators and world models for robotic manipulation, and developing real-to-sim-to-real methods to build robust and performant robotic manipulation systems. Previously, I was a research intern at World Labs and SceniX. My research is partially supported by the Qualcomm Innovation Fellowship.

Before Columbia, I received my Bachelor's degree from Tsinghua University (Yao Class). I am fortunate to receive mentorship from Changxi Zheng and Kris Hauser during my Ph.D. study, and Xiaolong Wang, Yang Gao, and Li Yi during my undergrad.
